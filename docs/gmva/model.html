DEC is the knowledge and communication layer.

DEC acts as a communications bus between Functions and Management, a knowledge store, and an access-control mechanism.

DEC is also economically a Function: it can have Income, Purchase Costs, Refining and Profit/Loss.

The mathematical form

I(p) = p · q(p)

P(p) = cP · q(p)

R(p) = cR · q(p)

Π(p) = I − P − R = q(p)[p − cP − cR]

The Controller is external.

The Controller may change price, Purchase Costs, Refining, transaction definition, volume, capacity or Function scope and then observe the resulting organization-level economics. Higher Refining can also enable a higher price.
